**Papers accepted at ICLR 2026**: 
Our lab will present 4 papers at ICLR 2026 in Rio (1 full paper, and 3 workshop papers), on control-theoretic in-training compression of SSMs, frequency-aware flow matching, structured pruning of (gated) deltanet models, and data-free mixed-precision quantization of MoEs.
